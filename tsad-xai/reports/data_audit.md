## 8. −999 sentinels (follow-up F3)

exact: `x == -999`; near: `|x + 999| ≤ 3.0 × MAD(train prefix)` (near ⊇ exact). GT interval from the active convention. `spike_n` / `max_k`: how many exact points are local outliers **in their own series** (`|x[i] − median_w| / MAD_w > k`, w = 51, k = 10.0) and the largest such ratio. Twin check (F3-2): the plain series' exact indices tested with the same rule in each DISTORTED/NOISE twin of the same `name_group`; format `twin:status(confirmed/absent/length_mismatch)`. Exclusion indices (exact, not in_gt): `data/derived/sentinels/<num>.npy`. **No series was modified.**

**Finding (measured, drives D-F3-2):** the near tolerance is unusable on this archive — see the `tol` and `range` columns: the series that contain −999 are 12-bit-scale signals (about ±2047) with training-prefix MAD in the hundreds, so tol = 3.0×MAD spans a large part of the signal range. `near` therefore counts ordinary samples (6,212,609 points in 68 series, 45 of them with no exact hit at all).
